About Our Initiative

Kartavya is a platform built on a simple belief: every citizen has a role in building a better India.

We connect real-world challenges faced by our communities with the young minds, universities, researchers, and industries capable of solving them. Citizens can share the problems around them, while students and innovators work together to turn those challenges into practical solutions.

By combining the power of India's youth, technology, innovation, and collaboration, Kartavya aims to support the spirit of Make in India and empower the next generation to create solutions for the nation.

Because the future of India is not just something we wait for — it is something we build together.
